About

LabLink

LabLink matches student research candidates to labs by combining a structured rule-based scorer with an AI semantic model, so fit is judged on more than a single keyword.

The problem

Finding meaningful research opportunities is often a challenge for students and early-stage researchers. The usual approach is cold emailing faculty members, which leads to limited responses and little insight into what a lab is actually working on. At the same time, research labs struggle to identify qualified candidates with the right academic background, skill set, and research interests. This gap hinders scientific productivity, limits access to valuable research experience, and delays biomedical research progress.

How it works

Every candidate and lab is described through two data models. Labs are described by their techniques, research fields, and aims. Candidates are described by their skills, field interests, goals, and a short research statement in their own words.

1

Structured matching

Techniques, fields, and goals are compared directly, with each type of match weighted by how strong a signal it is, from an exact field match down to a related, adjacent field.

2

AI semantic matching

The candidate's research statement and the lab's description are compared for meaning, not just shared words, so related ideas expressed differently still score as a strong match.

The AI layer

The free-text research statement is sent to the Cohere Embed API, which converts the text into a numerical representation of its meaning. The same happens for each lab's description. The two are then compared for similarity, and that score is blended with the structured score to produce the final match.
